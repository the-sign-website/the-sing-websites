A little about us

Who Are We?

We are two young people from Bad Salzuflen, Germany, and we are building The-Sing Websites as a small side project. We are both 16 years old, which means we are still learning, still growing, and still figuring things out step by step. We started from zero, with no big agency background and no expectations that everything has to be perfect from day one. But we are serious about what we do, and we care about creating websites that are clear, professional, and easy to understand. This is a side hustle for us — built with passion, curiosity, and a lot of motivation to improve every day.
